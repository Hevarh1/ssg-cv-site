From June 2020 to January 2022 I volunteered at the Advanced Robotics Lab at the University of Malaya, under the academic guidance of Prof. Loo Chu Kiong.

- I programmed software pipelines with PyTorch and TensorFlow for smart glasses, to support computer vision tasks.
- I developed and tested a lightweight facial recognition application optimised for Android mobile processors.
