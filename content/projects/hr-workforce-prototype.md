I designed and built the complete prototype for an HR conference demonstration. It covers employee daily-log submission, HR-defined evaluation criteria, fingerprint-connected attendance, score evaluation, and AI-assisted suggestions for areas to improve.

It connects attendance and activity data into one workflow, to show how structured data can support workforce oversight and performance conversations.

This is a test and demo system for conference attendees. It is not a production deployment or an ongoing organisational platform.
