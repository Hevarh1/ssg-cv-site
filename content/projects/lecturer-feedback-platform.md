I led the development of a web-based lecturer feedback platform for Salahaddin University, serving over 20,000 active students.

- I designed role-based access control (RBAC), analytical dashboards for administrators, and secure submission flows.
- I prevented duplicate record submissions and fixed scaling and responsive-layout problems on mobile browsers.

Built with Laravel, MySQL, Livewire and Tailwind CSS, from February 2025.
